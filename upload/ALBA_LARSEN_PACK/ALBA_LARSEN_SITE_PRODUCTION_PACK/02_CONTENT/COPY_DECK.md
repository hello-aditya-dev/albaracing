## Intro

Display:
`RACING IS THE BEGINNING.`

Body:
Alba Larsen came to racing later than many of the drivers around her. The years since have moved quickly: Danish karting, FIA Girls on Track, Formula 4, F1 Academy and now the Scuderia Ferrari Driver Academy. Around the racing sits a wider world of performance, fashion and G.I.R.L., the community she founded to help more girls find a way onto the grid.
