## Prototype mode

Keep:
`Independent website concept — not affiliated with or endorsed by Alba Larsen or associated organizations.`

Set:
`robots = noindex,nofollow`

Avoid:
- paid advertising using third-party marks
- pretending the URL is official
- contact forms that send to Alba without management approval
- downloadable press assets copied from media sites

## Official conversion checklist

Before removing the disclosure:
1. written approval from management
2. approved brand files
3. approved photo library
4. approved partner logos
5. approved partner relationship wording
6. approved biography/copy
7. approved contact routes
8. privacy/cookie/legal review as appropriate
